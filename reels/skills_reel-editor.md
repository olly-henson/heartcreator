# Skill: Reel Auto-Editor + Poster

Turns Olly's raw iPhone Reel recordings into finished Reels in his exact on-screen style, matches each to its Hub calendar hook, and (after his one approval) schedules it on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube Shorts through GHL.
Tools: `reels\reel_editor.py` (the edit), `reels\upload_server.py` (phone upload + review page, port 8800, plus the GHL poster thread), `reels\ghl_poster.py` (slots, GHL upload, posts, posted check).
Reference pair (the style to match): `reels\sample\Raw.MOV` (raw) and `reels\sample\Final.mp4` (Olly's own edit). Both stay local and are never committed.

## Goal
Olly only records. His day: push hooks from the content generator to the Hub calendar → set each day's Reel number in the day panel (0-10, default 4) → record from the Hub **Record** tab (hook + CTA keyword, nothing else) → bulk upload every raw video in any order on the upload page → review each under **To approve** and tap **✓ Approve and schedule** once. Everything else is automatic: matching, editing, captions per platform, scheduling, and the statuses Draft → Recorded → Scheduled → Published on the Hub calendar.

## Process / Steps
1. **Study the reference first** when changing the look: pull frames from `Final.mp4` at the same timestamps as the test render (caption band y 240-560 on 1080x1920). Match measurements, don't guess them.
2. **Transcribe** with faster-whisper `small.en` on the CPU (numpy audio; PyAV breaks on this PC). Cached as `<raw>.words.json` (~5 min for a 3-min clip, re-edits skip it). Punctuation retry with an `initial_prompt` if the result is a run-on.
3. **Match to the calendar:** `reel_editor.py RAW OUT --words-only` prints `FIRSTWORDS "<first 30 words>"`; `match_score()` = share of the hook's words said in order at the start; 0.5+ = match (tested 1.0 right vs 0.17 wrong). Candidates = unpublished calendar Reels (Hub GET, -21..+14 days, skipped ones excluded) not already taken. Olly can pick/change the hook from the card's dropdown (re-edit ~2 min). **A re-take won't match / its hook is missing from the dropdown:** another item (often a deleted dud) still claims it, or the Hub stage is stuck on "edited". Check `ready\reels.json` for other items with that `match` and the Hub stage (GET /hub-reels on the laptop), then POST `/match/<id>` with the exact calendar title (emoji included) and wait for `ready`. Since 2026-11-08 Delete frees the hook (`hub_stage(match, "")`) when no other item holds it.
4. **Trim the start and end only.** Start = `speech_onset()`: first 60ms run above THIS clip's own noise floor (3x the 20th-percentile level, or 25% of the way to the peak) near whisper's first word, minus 0.05s. The hook must be heard in the very first moment. Search window = first word -1.5s to +2.5s (2026-11-11: whisper put the first word 0.7s early, the old +0.6s window missed his voice and kept 0.7s of silence). Check: voice within 0.05s of frame 0 on every Reel. End = last word + 0.35s.
5. **Fit the length:** every Reel ≤ 179s (YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels limit is 3:00). `fit_length()` shortens the longest pauses down to 0.25s, longest first, only until it fits; never words, never the first 4s (hook) or last 8s (CTA). Still over: an even speed-up (≤ a few %). `remap()` moves word times so captions stay in sync. Result length is saved in the plan (`length`, `speed`).
6. **Hook text** = the words he actually SAID (`merge_hook()`): misheard words take the calendar's spelling ("While" → "Why"), words he skipped or added follow his speech, the calendar's end emoji is kept, trailing punctuation dropped, first word capitalised. A fast "How to" comes out as "Have a" / "How a" / "How do": word-by-word matching can't catch it (the words look nothing alike), so `merge_hook()` maps that opener to "How to" whenever the calendar hook starts with "How to" (2026-11-11, "Have a stop doubting…" was on screen and booked to post). After every batch, read each hook against its calendar title; any hook that doesn't read as English gets fixed before it posts.
7. **Hook layout** (`hook_fit()` / `hook_lines()`, rewritten 2026-11-06): ALWAYS an inverted pyramid: each line at least as wide as the one below it, the bottom line counting its end emoji, every line within HOOK_MAX 780px (phones crop ~100px off each side). Order of tries: (1) two lines at 66px, the most balanced pyramid wins; (2) two lines shrinking 2px at a time, but only down to PYRAMID_MIN_PX 48 (slightly smaller is fine, 40px was "too small"); (3) a hook too long for that (about 10+ words) goes to THREE lines, still a pyramid, at the biggest size that fits.
8. **Captions:** white rounded box, bold black, 2-4 words (22 chars max); new caption at a sentence end or a 0.35s pause; hidden during 0.6s+ pauses; final full stops stripped.
9. **Green (#10D05D):**
   - Every mention of "intellectualization" and "emotional resonance" (s/z spellings both match, always shown as Olly's US "intellectualization"). Own caption box when he names it ("called X") or says it as its own sentence; green in line otherwise.
   - CTA keywords (feeling, emotion, video, attract, scale) when "comment" / "DM" / "message" / "word" came up to 12 words before, across caption boxes; comma after them removed. Green both times (after "comment" and after "DM").
   - **ALWAYS two green FEELINGs at the end (Olly 2026-11-11: "I can't go through every single video checking ... otherwise I might as well just do the editing myself").** Three guards in reel_editor.py: (a) `fix_words` turns a misheard DM ("hear me", "dare me", `DM_MISHEARD`) into "DM me" and "comment, feel" into "comment feeling", only next to a CTA cue; (b) the 12-word / "the word X" window ("send me a DM if you want to keep it private with the word feeling" = DM 11 words back); (c) a safety net in `plan()`: any comment/DM cue in the last 80 words with no green keyword after it forces the nearest "feel…" word to green FEELING, and logs "WARNING green safety net" if there's none. After every batch, run the green audit (re-plan each .words.json, count greens in the last 40 words) and re-make any Reel without both.
10. **Render:** PIL PNGs of the caption strip only, one ffmpeg overlay at y=240 (keep-segments trimmed + concatenated when pauses were shortened). 1080x1920, 30fps, H.264 Quick Sync, AAC.
11. **Captions per platform:** the Hub's built-in Perfect Reel captions (mirrored to KV `reelcaptions`), exactly as laid out per platform. Only the set changes: it follows the keyword he SAYS at the end (`spoken_offer()`: ATTRACT → attract, FEELING/EMOTION → scale, VIDEO → video), else the calendar's CTA. `VIDEO TITLE GOES HERE` (Facebook line 1) = the on-screen hook.
12. **Check before telling Olly it's ready:** read the hook word by word, grab frame 0 (hook + emoji), a frame at each green word, and the first 1s of audio (voice from ~0.0s). Confirm length ≤ 179s. For a whole batch, run the audit in one script over every ready/scheduled item (Olly 2026-11-11: "I can't go through every single video checking"): hook vs calendar title, two green FEELINGs in the last 40 caption words, every TERM green, voice ≤0.05s. Report which Reels failed and why, fix them, then say it's done.
13. **Schedule on approval** (`gp.schedule()`): book the slot (an item that already has `slot` set keeps it, so a slot written on a ready item before Olly approves is where it books), upload the mp4 to GHL `/medias/upload-file`, create one `scheduled` post per platform (IG reel, FB reel, TikTok post PUBLIC_TO_EVERYONE, YouTube `youtubePostDetails {title: hook ≤100, privacyLevel public, type short}`), Hub stage → scheduled. Unschedule deletes the unpublished posts and keeps the upload.
14. **Slots** (`SPREAD` in ghl_poster.py, Windhoek UTC+2, posting day 06:00 → 06:00, US emphasis: Windhoek = ET+7): a timetable per day count, e.g. 4 = 15:00, 19:00, 23:00, 02:00. Count = max(the day's Hub number, Reels on that calendar day), capped at `MAX_A_DAY = 6` (Olly 2026-11-07, was 10). A matched Reel books its own calendar day, earliest free slot ≥ 20 min ahead, unless that day already has 6 booked: then it goes to the next day. A late or unmatched Reel tops up the first day below its number (rest days skipped).
15. **Posted check** every 10 min after the slot (`gp.check()`): GHL replaces a scheduled post when it publishes: the old one goes `deleted: true` (status still "scheduled") and a new post appears with `parentPostId` = old `_id`, its own status and the live link in `previewLink`. Follow the child; save links; Hub stage → posted (the Hub then sets Published + Video stats by itself).
