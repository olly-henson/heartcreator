How each figure is worked out

Email leads (quiz)
Leads captured through the free quiz funnel — a separate entry point from the About Page, tracked alongside it rather than folded in.
Active members (month end)
Typed in by hand each month — the total paying member count as of that month's end. Not calculated from the previous month, so a mistake in one month never throws off another.
Trial conversion rate
Free trials started ÷ About Page visits. How well the page turns browsers into trialists.
Trial → paid conversion
New paying members ÷ free trials started that month. How well the trial itself sells the community.
Visit → paid conversion
New paying members ÷ About Page visits. The full-funnel rate, start to finish.
Churn rate
Members lost ÷ that month's active members. Each month calculates this from its own two numbers only.
Retention rate
100% minus churn. The share of members who stuck around.
New member price
What someone signing up that month pays — $30/mo through August 2026, $99/mo (or $990/yr) from September 2026 onward.
MRR
Calculated automatically, but not as Active members × price. Every month's new members lock in at that month's own price and keep contributing it every month afterward — so a member who joined at $30 keeps paying $30 even once the price rises to $99 for new signups. Lost members are removed at the average rate across current members, since exactly which member left isn't tracked. The only figure on this page that isn't fully independent per month — it has to look back, because grandfathered pricing means you can't tell current MRR from one month's numbers alone.
Avg. revenue / member
MRR ÷ Active members. The true blended amount each member is worth this month, accounting for mixed pricing.
LTV
Avg. revenue per member ÷ churn rate. What an average member is worth over their full membership, based on what they're actually paying rather than the sticker price.

Monthly / Yearly / All time view
The glance cards at the top total up whichever months you're viewing — visits, leads, trials and new members are added together; active members, price and MRR use the most recent month with a real Active members count, so live current MRR stays correct even if a later month's Active members field just hasn't been filled in yet.
