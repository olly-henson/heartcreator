Why the transition feels easy

The one word does all the work. Once it's revealed, it gets reused to name the pain, then the result, so by the time the solution is named after it, the name feels like the obvious next step.

  1. Reveal the word in capitals: "IT'S SECURITY."
  2. Show who already has it and why it works: "It's simple psychology…"
  3. Name the pain after the word: "symptoms of low security… that's just what happens when you don't feel secure."
  4. Picture the result with the word: "With real security, how much easier…"
  5. Turn the word into the solution: "That's why I built The Security Blueprint", then one sentence on what it does.
  6. Repeat the word in what's inside: "Security Step #1…"

The solution name is just the word plus a simple container: Blueprint, Method, Map, Matrix, Formula or Code. Proof is left as placeholders in these examples, because the coaches aren't real.
