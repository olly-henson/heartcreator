# Funnel Overview — Heart Creator Community

> The strategic context for all content creation. Every piece of content has a job in this funnel. Read this before planning any content calendar or writing any copy.
>
> **Rebuilt 2026-07-29** — the previous version described a retired $5,000 1-2-1 "apply" funnel and a stale 10-step pitch sequence that no longer exist. Fulfilment Coaching (which the 1-2-1 offer moved to) is now itself fully retired — repo archived, local files deleted. Heart Creator Community is the only business and the only offer.

---

## The goal

Move the right people from content → free meditation → email nurture → Heart Creator Community (Skool, $30/mo or $300/yr, 7-day free trial).

There is no application funnel and no 1-2-1 offer anywhere in this business anymore.

---

## Entry points

Two ways someone enters the funnel:

1. **Instagram** — watches a Reel or Story, comments a keyword, ManyChat DMs the link
2. **Skool** — discovers the community through Skool's own discovery/search as cold traffic

YouTube is not part of Heart Creator marketing (see `CLAUDE.md` Active Platforms note — YouTube belonged to the now-retired Fulfilment Coaching business).

---

## Platform roles

### Instagram

**Job:** Hook people in fast, make them feel heard, drive either meditation downloads (list-building) or Community trial signups (conversion) depending on the Reel's Aim.

**Content tone:** Punchy, direct, hook-led, unfiltered/low-production. Current content strategy (confirmed 2026-07-29): up to 6 Reels/day, treated as a data-generation engine — post volume, watch what wins on views/engagement/leads, then go deeper on winning angles. *(Note: `CLAUDE.md`'s Active Platforms section still says "2 Reels/day" — that line is now stale and needs updating to match.)*

**Two CTA keywords — do not mix them up (confirmed 2026-07-29):**
- **HEART** → ManyChat sends the free Heart Activation Meditation link. Used on list-building Reels/Stories, posted at least once/day.
- **TRIAL** → ManyChat sends the Heart Creator Community / Skool 7-day free trial link. Used on trial-offer Reels, spoken CTA in-video + matching caption.

Live caption copy for both is kept in the **Reel Caption Bank** Notion page: `https://app.notion.com/p/Reel-Caption-Bank-3ac30e586a0d81ed8378c5e8c22ac9f1`

**Stories CTA:** "DM me the word HEART" (meditation) or "DM me COMMUNITY" (Skool signup link) — see `skills_instagram-stories.md`.

**Profile:** Link to meditation + link to Heart Creator Community in bio.

### Skool Community — Heart Creator Community

**Job:** Serve two audiences:
1. **Warm leads** who came through Instagram content and/or the email nurture sequence
2. **Cold discovery traffic** finding the page organically through Skool's own platform

**Pricing:** $30/month or $300/year, 7-day free trial (card required at signup, converts to paid automatically unless cancelled).

**What's inside:** The Creation Formula (the step-by-step system — renamed 2026-07-29 from "Heart Creator Program"), 24/7 community support, and the community itself. See `project_about_page_bio` memory for the exact live About Page copy.

**Page requirement:** Must work for both audiences. Cold traffic lands here with no prior context — the page needs to be self-contained, clear and compelling on its own.

## The email nurture sequence (live — v4, 2026-07-29)

Triggered by the "Meditation Download" tag in GHL once someone downloads the free meditation. Every other day. 10 emails, numbered 0-9, no gaps.

| Email | Job |
|---|---|
| 0 (Delivery) | Delivers the meditation link + sets expectations for the series. Asks for a "YES" reply — a deliverability play to improve open/inbox rates for the rest of the sequence and future broadcasts, not a hard content gate. |
| 1 | Recognition — names the frustration (tried everything, nothing's really happening) without blaming them |
| 2 | They're close — reframes the gap as a state problem (survival mode vs creative mode), not a technique problem |
| 3 | What's actually blocking them — mind/body/brainwaves/heart all need to align to create consciously |
| 4 | Proof — Olly's personal story (364.12% income increase, late 2018, via Heart Coherence) |
| 5 | Why most techniques/teachers don't help people actually *feel* it |
| 6 | The two-step framework — Step 1: Shift Out of Survival Mode Into Creative Mode, Step 2: Think About & Feel Your Future |
| 7 | Summary of everything covered so far |
| 8 | Pitch — The Creation Formula inside the Heart Creator Community, 7-day free trial |
| 9 | Transition — explicitly moves them onto the regular broadcast list, sets expectations (paid offers will appear, no pressure, easy unsubscribe) |

Full sequence content: `marketing/skills/skills_email-creation.md` (Section 7) and `marketing/email-sequences/Email Nurture Sequence - v4_.md`.

## Key rules for content creation

- Every piece of content must have a clear job in this funnel — awareness, education, or conversion
- Check the Aim field before writing a CTA — HEART for list-building, TRIAL for trial/conversion. Never mix them.
- The free meditation is the primary list-building lead magnet — protect it as the main CTA on list-building content
- Skool page must work as a standalone for cold traffic — never assume prior context
- No 1-2-1, no application funnel, no `$997/yr` or `$5,000` pricing anywhere — those are retired, don't resurrect them in new copy
