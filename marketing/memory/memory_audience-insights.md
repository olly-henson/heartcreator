# Audience Insights: The Heart Attractor Program

> What the audience tells us in their own answers. Content, emails and the generator draw from this.
> PUBLIC REPO: counts and themes only. No names, no quotes that could identify someone.
> Source order for all content (Olly, 2026-11-12): the best client (primary avatar) first, then the core argument, then good applicants, then other applicants and Feeling Finder answers. Never invent a pain point.
> Refresh: rerun the GHL pull (Feeling Finder + application fields) every 2 weeks and update the counts.

---

## Pull 1: 2026-11-03 (63 Feeling Finder results, 9 applications, 25 Oct to 3 Nov)

### 1. How they feel right now (Feeling Finder, 60 answers)
- **Worn out and losing hope = 43%:** impatience 9, hopelessness 9, exhaustion 8 (26 of 60)
- Next: doubt 5, loneliness 5, frustration 4, disappointment 3, worry 3, anxiety 3
- Almost nobody is angry or bitter. They're tired. Content that says "you're not broken, you're tired of waiting" lands better than fixing a flaw.
- Confirms the core message: **"I don't believe it will happen."**

### 2. The feeling they want (Feeling Finder, 63 answers)
- **Safety 13, belonging 10, love 8, peace 8** (39 of 63 = 62%)
- Joy 5, calm 3, contentment 3, gratitude 3. Excitement only 1.
- They want to feel safe and chosen, and that's the thing they've never had. Content angle: "the feeling you're chasing is safety." Keep it fun as well as safe (never say the right partner feels boring).

### 3. Who they want (intentions)
- **"Emotionally available" + "ready to commit" is far ahead of everything else** (~20 mentions combined)
- Then funny, kind, emotionally intelligent, ambitious, provider, loyal
- The fear underneath: attracting another unavailable or avoidant partner. Several applicants say this outright ("I fall for avoidants", "they don't stick around", "can't hold a long-term relationship").

### 4. Applicants (9)
- **7 of 9 single for more than 5 years**; 6 not really dating
- **They've tried everything:** therapy, inner child work, attachment styles, manifestation, meditation, books/courses, dating coaches, apps. Most ticked 4 to 7. Manifestation ticked by most, so "this isn't manifestation" matters.
- **Blaming circumstances:** 3 of 9 blame age, location or the "wrong country/area" ("the field gets smaller"). It's a belief they can't control, so it keeps them stuck.
- **The real question they ask:** "Is there still hope for me?" / "Will this work at my age?"
- What they think needs to change is vague or hopeless ("love myself", "accept uncertainty", "I don't know if I ever will").
- **Budget: 7 of 9 chose "Under $500"** (2 chose $500 to $1,000). Business flag for the $497/mo offer: see NEXT.md.
- Source: mostly Instagram (DM / comment keyword).

### Content angles from Pull 1 (use in emails, YT community, Reels)
1. **Tired, not broken:** you're exhausted from waiting, and that exhaustion is the state that keeps love away.
2. **"Is there still hope for me?"** Answer it head-on: age, years single and location don't decide it. Your emotional state does.
3. **Why you keep meeting unavailable people:** fear decides who you notice and who you're drawn to.
4. **You've tried everything (therapy, attachment styles, manifestation):** they work on the past or the wish. None of them train the feeling daily.
5. **Safety is the feeling you're after:** what it's like to feel safe and chosen, and how to feel it before they arrive.
6. **"The field gets smaller":** the circumstances story, and why it's a belief, not a fact.
7. **Impatience:** counting the years single (7 of 9 applicants single 5+ years) and what impatience tells your nervous system.
