## What it is

The Heart Attractor Program helps people who've done years of inner work attract a genuine, committed long-term relationship, by changing the one thing all that work never touched: **their emotional state around love**. Members join the community, work through The Attraction Formula (the trainings and the 10-minute daily meditation) and put it into action in their own dating lives, with Olly beside them every day.
