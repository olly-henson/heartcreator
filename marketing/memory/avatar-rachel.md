# Avatar — Rachel

> The primary creative anchor. Write every piece of content, every email, every video script as if speaking directly to Rachel. She represents a broader demographic of seekers but keeping her in mind sharpens the message.

---

## Who Rachel is

- High-paid professional, works in tech — very good at her job but doesn't see herself there long-term
- Driven, positive, always thinking and talking about her vision for her life
- Busy but with a strong spiritual side
- Single, late 30s, based in the US

## What Rachel wants to create

Living and enjoying her life with her amazing partner — a life partner, a husband.

## What Rachel believes

She believes in manifestation completely. She has read widely and gone deep — Joe Dispenza, Neville Goddard, Abraham Hicks, Reality Transurfing, and more.

She believes attracting the relationship she wants is possible. She's tried the techniques — vision boards, scripting, affirmations.

But she cannot make it land.

## What's actually going on for Rachel

- She scripts, affirms, visualises the relationship — but nothing sticks
- She gets fixated on one person, replaying and overanalysing every interaction
- She's watched someone she wanted pull away in real time — and known, in that moment, it was her own neediness pushing it away
- She rides the cycle of hope and heartbreak every time an attempt doesn't work
- She's tried "just detach and stop caring" and "just stay positive" too — neither has worked either
- She quietly doubts whether she's even worthy of the relationship she wants
- She's getting bored of content that doesn't address the elephant in the room: *why isn't this working for my love life specifically?*
- She doesn't feel heard

## What she needs to understand

1. Her efforts are valid. She is not the problem.
2. It's not about finding the right technique, being more consistent, detaching and caring less, or staying positive.
3. She has Internal Working Models — unconscious beliefs about her own worthiness and whether others can be trusted, formed in childhood — running the show. Beliefs like "I'm not worthy," "I have to earn love," or "closeness is dangerous" are what's actually driving the chasing, the fixating, the self-sabotage.
4. The Attraction Formula Meditation rewrites these internal working models: Heart Coherence calms her nervous system past the analytical mind, then she imprints the qualities of the partner she wants, feels the emotions of already being with them, and imprints new beliefs and a self-image aligned with the relationship.
5. She doesn't force this by trying harder, and she doesn't fix it by giving up and detaching. She rewrites the beliefs running underneath it.

## The core argument for Rachel

**You don't force this. You don't detach from it. You rewrite the beliefs running underneath it.**

---

## The broader audience Rachel represents

Rachel is a demographic and psychographic anchor, not a single person. The broader audience shares:

- Deep investment in manifestation, already believes reality creation works
- Trying specifically to attract a life partner — not a broad self-improvement journey
- Real frustration that belief and technique aren't translating into a relationship
- A pattern of chasing, fixating, and watching people pull away — followed by hope and heartbreak on repeat
- Quiet self-worth doubts underneath the frustration at the mechanics

They are not sceptics. They are **believers stuck at the door.**

- "You don't force this. You don't detach from it. You rewrite the beliefs running underneath it."
- "You have internal working models — beliefs about your worthiness and whether others can be trusted — formed in childhood, still running the show."
- "The Attraction Formula Meditation rewrites your internal working models."

---

## What Rachel is wary of

She has tried many things and been disappointed many times. She is cautious of:
- Another programme that becomes just more information
- Overpromised results that don't last
- Being sold to before being understood
- Feeling patronised or talked down to

Content that acknowledges this directly — rather than ignoring it — builds far more trust than content that simply makes promises.
