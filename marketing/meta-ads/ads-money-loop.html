How ads end up paying for themselves

With a $497/month or $2,500/year program you don't need ads to be cheap. You need each dollar to come back as more than a dollar, fast enough to fund the next one. Once that happens, scaling is just turning the budget up.

4 · Scaling

Climb one step at a time

Each step only unlocks when the one before has proved itself. Raising the budget too fast resets Meta's learning and costs go up.

  1. $10/dayNow

    Prove the ads get sign-ups

    3 ads, cold audience only. Judge on cost per sign-up and whether anyone applies.

    Move up when: sign-ups under $10 and at least 1 application
  2. $20–30/dayMonth 2

    Prove the ads get sales

    Replace the weakest ad every 2 weeks with your best new Reel.

    Move up when: cost per sale under $650
  3. $30–50/dayMonth 3

    Add retargeting

    10–20% of spend shown to people who watched, engaged or visited /training but didn't apply. These ads go straight to the application.

    Move up when: cash back in month one covers spend
  4. +20% every 3–4 daysAfter that

    Turn it up while it holds

    Keep raising while cost per sale stays under $650. If it jumps, step back one level and refresh the ads.

5 · Why the price matters

The most you can pay to win one member

A rule most advertisers use: a member should be worth at least 3 times what it cost to get them. So the price decides how much you're allowed to spend per sale.

Old $30/month trial · about 3 months$90 value → max $30 per sale

No ad can find a buyer for $30. This is why paid ads never made sense before.

The Heart Attractor Program · about $2,000$2,000 value → max $650 per sale

Twenty times more room. Ads only need to find one buyer in every few hundred clicks.

6 · Tracking

How each ad is scored

Two places, one sum. Meta tells you what you spent. Your Hub tells you what came back, credited to the ad each person first came from, however long they take.

From Meta Ads ManagerWhat you spent

Per ad, per day, to the cent.

From GHL + the HubWhat came back

Sign-ups, applications and sales, each marked with the ad they first came from (tracked link + the permanent meta ad tag).

Spend ÷ sign-ups from the ad = cost per sign-upAim: under $8
Spend ÷ sales from the ad = cost per saleAim: under $650

A slow buyer still counts

  1. Day 0
    Sees the boosted Reel and comments FEELING. ManyChat sends the tracked link.
  2. Day 0
    Does the Feeling Finder. GHL records “first came from: Meta ad, feel confident Reel” and adds the meta ad tag for good.
    Meta's own report stops watching about 7 days after the click.
  3. Week 4
    Applies from one of your daily emails. GHL records “applied from: daily email” separately, so the ad isn't overwritten.
  4. Weeks 5–10
    Plan of action, sales video, goes quiet for a while.
  5. Week 10
    Joins.
The Hub credits this sale to the Meta ad.Meta's report would show nothing, because the sale came weeks after its 7-day window. That's why the ads are judged in the Hub, not in Meta.
7 · 12 months, everyone pays monthly, budget grows

How members stack up over a year

Here every member pays $497 a month and nobody pays yearly. Each month the ads bring in new members, and some existing members leave. The ones who stay keep paying, so income builds month after month. As the income comes in, the ad budget goes up.

New members each month and the ad budget come from your numbers in section 2. Change them there and this updates.

Paid coaching memberships commonly lose somewhere around 10–20% of members a month. This is a rough range, not hard data. We start at 15% until your own members show the real number.
