How each figure is worked out

What you fill in
Four numbers a month: new monthly members ($497/month), new annual members ($2,500/year), monthly members who cancelled, and annual members who cancelled or didn't renew.
Members
A running total: last month's members, plus this month's new ones, minus this month's cancellations. Split into monthly and annual.
MRR
Monthly members × $497, plus annual members × $208.33 ($2,500 ÷ 12). Annual is pro-rated so MRR shows the real monthly revenue, not the upfront payment.
Churn
Members lost that month ÷ members at the start of the month. The first month shows "—" because there was nobody to lose yet.
Retention
100% minus churn. The share of members who stayed.
Avg. revenue / member
MRR ÷ active members.
LTV
Avg. revenue per member ÷ churn. What an average member is worth over their whole membership. Shows "—" until someone has cancelled.
Monthly / Yearly / All time view
Sign-ups and cancellations add up across the months you're viewing. Members and MRR come from the latest month. Churn and retention are averaged across the months.
Lock 🔓 / 🔒
Greys out a finished month so you can't overwrite it by accident. Locking doesn't save on its own — click Save when you're ready.
