What's in the way

There's nothing to fix. You're stuck in your head.

You've tried . None of it was wasted, but the list of things to fix never ends. Your friends who settled down didn't fix every part of themselves first.

What's going on is something called intellectualization. When love feels uncomfortable, your mind jumps in to analyse and fix, to get away from the feeling. Your inner voice gets louder and more critical and you end up stuck in your head.

The problem
Intellectualization
Stuck in your head. Busy mind. Worrying, overthinking, scanning for answers.
The solution
Emotional resonance
Back in your heart. Calm mind. Present, light and playful. Just being you.

So we need to get you out of the worry, the overthinking and the belief that you need to fix yourself.

That's what lets you meet someone and have things play out naturally and enjoyably, with a genuine connection, just like the friends and family you know who have the kind of relationship you want too.
