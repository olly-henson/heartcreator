# DM plan setting: FEELING commenters → plan of action request

Built 2026-11-12. For Olly or a DM setter. Turns people who asked for the Feeling Finder into plan of action requests.
The goal of the DM is NOT an application or a sale. It's one thing: they fill in the plan form.
After the form, it's the normal sales flow from Flow v3 step 4 (`skills_applicant-looms.md`): make their plan, send it with a voice note, video, join link.

**Form link:** `ollyhenson.com/plan-dm` (form = funnel/sections/plan-request.html, page /plan, not live yet). Asks: name/email, where things are, how long, relationship wanted, tried, feelings about love, "once I…", priority, profession, WhatsApp (country worked out from its code + browser timezone) (no Instagram username: Olly 2026-11-12).

---

## The flow at a glance

1. They comment FEELING → ManyChat sends the Feeling Finder link (unchanged).
2. **ManyChat, ~2 hours after they click "yes" (automatic):** the check-in below.
3. They reply → you take over by hand with the 5 steps: Where things are → Goals → Challenges → Mini-value → Invitation.
4. They fill in the form → their card appears in Hub > Sales → plan as normal.

## The rules

- **One question per message.** Short. Then wait.
- **Use their words back to them.** If they say "I keep attracting unavailable men", that's the phrase you use from then on.
- **Listen more than you teach.** Mini-value is two or three lines, not a lesson.
- **Never pitch the program in the DM.** No price, no "apply". The only offer is the plan.
- **No reply = no chase.** One check-in only (ManyChat sends it). If they go quiet mid-chat, one nudge after 2 days max, then leave it.
- **Not a fit = kind exit, no form.** In a relationship and trying to fix it, wants an ex back, wants texting scripts: answer warmly, point them to the daily emails / Feeling Finder, stop there.
- Voice notes are great once they're chatting (steps 3 to 5). Text for the first two.
- No dashes as separators, no comma before "and"/"or", "program" not "programme".

## Message 1 (automatic, ManyChat): the check-in

> Hey, hope that link came through all right 🙏
>
> How are things for you right now with love and dating?

(Adding "with love and dating" stops "good thanks!" replies and gets them talking about the real thing.)

---

## Step 1: Where things are

They've just answered the check-in. Reflect, then dig one level deeper.

> Thanks for sharing that, I really appreciate it.
>
> How long have you been single now?

If they already said how long:

> And are you dating at the moment, or taking a bit of a break from it?

(Real data: 6 of 9 applicants aren't really dating. Don't assume they are.)

## Step 2: Goals

> And if things went exactly how you wanted, what kind of relationship would you love to be in?

If the answer is vague ("just someone nice"), one follow-up:

> Love that. What would it feel like day to day being with them?

(Most say safety, belonging, love, peace: the Feeling Finder top 4. That's their target feeling. Note it, you'll use it in Step 4.)

## Step 3: Challenges

> What do you feel has been getting in the way so far?

Then:

> And what have you tried already?

Listen for the patterns (all from real members and applicants):
- They've done the work (therapy, attachment styles, healing, manifestation, meditation) and it helped everywhere except love.
- They keep being drawn to unavailable or inconsistent people.
- They're tired of waiting, not broken. Impatience, hopelessness, exhaustion.
- "Is there still hope for me?" / age, location, "the field gets smaller".
- Fear of being hurt again keeps the old stories running.

## Step 4: Mini-value

Two or three lines, tied to what they just said. Pick the one that matches.

**They've done loads of work (most common):**
> That makes so much sense. Most people I speak to have done loads of work on themselves and it's helped in every area except love.
>
> But think about your friends in happy relationships. Most of them didn't do all that work first. They just felt a connection with someone.
>
> It's not that something needs fixing. Part of you just doesn't *feel* it can happen yet, and that's what we can change.

**They keep attracting unavailable people:**
> That's really common, and it's not a flaw in you.
>
> When part of you is braced for it not to work, that feeling decides who you notice and who you're drawn to.
>
> Change how you feel about love day to day, and who you're drawn to changes with it.

**They're tired / losing hope / "is there still hope for me":**
> I hear you, and honestly that exhaustion makes total sense after this long.
>
> Years single, age and where you live don't decide this. How you feel about love does, and that's something you can train.

**They mention the feeling they want (from Step 2 / the Feeling Finder):**
> And that feeling you mentioned, [their feeling], that's the one. The more you feel it now, before they arrive, the more it starts to feel real that it can happen.

## Step 5: Invitation (the plan)

> Would it help if I put together a plan of action for you? Basically what I'd do if I was in your shoes, and how I'd help you meet [their words: "someone emotionally available"].
>
> No cost, it's just a few questions so I can make it about you.

If yes:

> Amazing. Fill this in and I'll put it together for you 👇
>
> ollyhenson.com/plan-dm
>
> Once it's done I'll send it over to you on WhatsApp.

If they hesitate / ask what it is:

> It's a short personal plan, a few pages. Where you are now, where you want to be and the steps I'd take to get you there. You can keep it either way.

## After the form

1. Their card lands in Hub > Sales, tagged as a DM plan request, stage **Plan requested**.
2. Reply in the DM straight away: "Got it, thank you! I'll get your plan over to you shortly 🙏"
3. Hub > Sales > card > 📄 Plan of action → check answers → personal note → Make their link.
4. Send the link on WhatsApp (their number is on the card; no number = in the Instagram DM) + voice note: "Here's the plan of action I've put together for you, that we'll use to help you meet this special person. Let me know your thoughts once you've checked it out."
5. From here it's Flow v3 steps 5 to 7 (`skills_applicant-looms.md`): wait for them to read it, answer questions, their own video when they ask how to start, join link.

## Form rules (Olly's corrections, 2026-11-12)
- The form is a request for a free plan, never an application: title "Get Your Personal Plan Of Action To Attract Your Perfect Relationship", hello "If you'd really like to know exactly what you need to do to attract the relationship you want, just answer the following questions. I'll send you a free step-by-step plan of what you need to do to make it happen."
- Never ask what can be worked out: country comes from the WhatsApp code (+ the browser timezone). No country/city question.
- No Instagram username field (Olly: not needed). WhatsApp is required: the plan goes on WhatsApp.
- Profession is one box, framed for them: "So I can fit your plan around your day, what do you do professionally?"
- No investment or "information vs implementation" questions on a free plan (it would feel salesy; the video covers price).
- Previews must let Olly click through without filling anything in (only while the webhook is empty).
- Send the short link ollyhenson.com/plan-dm (Hub Links, top group), never the long UTM link.
- ManyChat check-in runs on the IG DM + comment FEELING automations only (story reply one skipped: hardly used).
