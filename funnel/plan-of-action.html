The Real Problem: Intellectualization

Intellectualization is the psychiatrist's term for using your analytical mind to try to fix yourself, in order to escape the discomfort of not finding someone.

It keeps your mind busy: worrying, overthinking and searching for the right 'thing' that will finally make it happen.

But it becomes a trap. There's always something else to fix, so you never truly believe it can happen for you.

Linked to a fight or flight brainwave pattern: high beta.

The Solution

Emotional Resonance

The Solution: Emotional Resonance

Emotional resonance is conditioning your body to feel as if you're already in the relationship you want.

You feel like you're already enough and you truly believe this is possible.

In this state you meet people as your authentic self. That's where a genuine emotional connection can happen.

Linked to a calmer brainwave pattern: alpha.

So we need to get you out of the worry, the overthinking and the belief that you need to fix yourself.

Then things play out naturally, like they did for the friends and family you know who have what you want.
