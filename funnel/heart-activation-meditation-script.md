# Heart Activation Meditation — Guided Script (Final)

> Target length: ~5:33 (matches previous version). To be recorded in Audacity over heart resonance music.
> Style: spoken intro + one demonstrated breath cycle, then silent practice (music only), then closing.

---

## [0:00 – 0:35] Welcome

Welcome to this Heart Activation Meditation. This meditation is going to help you regulate your nervous system, feel more like yourself and create a sense of wholeness within you. To put you in the right energy to attract your ideal partner, and a better quality of life.

Find a comfortable position, somewhere safe and quiet where you won't be disturbed for the next few minutes. Close your eyes, and let your body settle.

---

## [0:35 – 2:05] Setup — walked through once

We're going to activate your heart together.

Start by placing a hand over the centre of your chest. Just let your attention settle there.

Now we're going to breathe into your heart for six seconds. Breathe in slowly... one thousand and one, one thousand and two, one thousand and three, one thousand and four, one thousand and five, one thousand and six.

Hold for a moment at the top. No strain, just a soft pause.

And now breathe out, slowly, releasing the breath from your heart... one thousand and one, one thousand and two, one thousand and three, one thousand and four, one thousand and five, one thousand and six.

Let yourself reset. And that's it. That's the whole practice.

From here, just keep that same rhythm going on your own. In for six, a gentle pause, out for six, and reset. I'm going to go quiet now and let you settle into it. I'll meet you at the end.

---

## [2:05 – 4:50] Silent practice

~2:45 of silence — music/heart resonance track only. Roughly 9–10 natural breath cycles at this pace. No voice.

---

## [4:50 – 5:33] Closing

Welcome back.

Take a moment to notice how you feel right now, compared to how you felt a few minutes ago.

That shift, that lightness, that's your heart, activated. Carry it with you.

When you're ready, gently open your eyes.

---

## Recording notes
- Eyes closed throughout — intro line now states this directly (not optional), plus "somewhere safe and quiet."
- Counting uses full "one thousand and X" phrasing throughout, matching the on-page step text — deliberately slower pacing so neither Olly nor the listener rushes the breath.
- No mid-practice cueing — after the setup, stays silent until the closing so people can settle into their own rhythm without interruption.
