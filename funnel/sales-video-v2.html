Sales Video v2

Our sales video rebuilt on Frankie Fihn's Loom method, for The Heart Attractor Program. It replaces the current master video (the map). About 6 minutes. Freestyle it: the lines are example wording, not a script to read.

What we're taking from Frankie

  • Sell the outcome, never the program. Nobody wakes up wanting "a community and a meditation". They want someone to settle down and build a life with. Open on that.
  • One specific example beats big claims. Show one real person's result and one picture of the life they want, not "hundreds of members".
  • Evidence makes it believable. A real member message on screen does more than anything you can say about yourself. Keep your intro to one sentence.
  • "This works for you if…" is how the video screens people, so you never need a call. Say it early and again at the price.
  • Price formula: the investment is ___, for that you get ___, it works well if ___. The thing they get has to feel worth more than the money.
  • Show exactly how to buy. Click through the join page on screen and say what happens after they pay.
  • Pictures on every beat. If you're talking a lot, it's because you're not showing enough.

Your decisions

1. Follow-ups after the videoYour rule since 1 Nov is no follow-ups after a Loom. Frankie does follow up, but never "did you see my video?". Each follow-up goes one step deeper, as if you're already working together.

My recommendation: one follow-up 2 days after they watch, value only, no ask. "Hey [Name], I was thinking about what you said about wanting [Wants]. The first thing we'd do is get clear on how you'd feel with them. Try this tonight: close your eyes for 2 minutes and picture an ordinary Sunday with them." Then stop.

2. Personal videos for the next 5Frankie personalises the first 5 to 10 until people start saying yes, then switches to one video. You moved to one master video on 5 Nov.

My recommendation: record personal versions for the next 5 applicants, putting their own words on slides 1 and 5. The Hub's mind map already holds their answers. Then re-record the master from what landed.

3. Loom or unlisted YouTubeLoom tells you when someone has watched, which is what a deeper follow-up needs. YouTube doesn't.

My recommendation: Loom, if you go with decision 1. Otherwise keep YouTube.
