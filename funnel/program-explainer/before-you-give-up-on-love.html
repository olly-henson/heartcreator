Before You Give Up On Finding Love, Try This

Why nothing has worked so far, what's really going on and 3 steps to change it.

Is This You?

You really want to find someone to settle down with. Someone to build your life with. That could include marriage. It could include children.

You want someone who's fun, committed, loyal, attractive and funny. Whatever it is for you.

But right now dating feels transactional and empty. No matter what you do, things aren't changing. And you're exhausted.

What's Really Going On

You're stuck in something called intellectualization. It's the psychiatrist's term.

When we worry emotionally about something in our life, we move up into our head. Our brainwaves shift from a calm alpha, theta and low beta pattern and we get stuck in high beta.

In high beta the analytical mind gets super loud. That inner voice is constantly worrying, catastrophizing and stressing that you can't meet someone.

Why The Usual Fixes Don't Work

In that mode, we think something is wrong with us. So we look for things to fix:

  • "If I learn my attachment style, I'll know what to do."
  • "If I learn how to text someone, I'll know what to do."
  • "If I learn how to detach, I'll know what to do."

But no matter what you've done, you don't feel inside like it's going to happen. You don't believe it. And believing comes from feeling.

The Missing Piece: Emotional Resonance

Think about the friends and family who met their person easily. They all say the same thing about how they knew this person was right.

It was how they felt. The emotional connection. That's called emotional resonance.

That's what you need to develop to get over the worry that it isn't going to happen. You train yourself to feel emotionally like you're already with your partner.

The 3 Steps

  1. Get Clear On Exactly What You Want

    Write 3 or 4 intentions. Use a pen and paper, not your phone or laptop. Do you want someone loyal, committed, who would give everything for you, who's massively supportive of your ambitions? Make it personal to you.

  2. Work Out The Emotions

    If all those things were true, what emotions would you feel? Take some time and write them down.

  3. The most important step

    Train Your Body To Feel Them Every Day

    Practice feeling these emotions on command. If it's peace or safety, you train yourself to feel it in your heart and in your body.

What Changes

It takes away all the strain about it happening, because you already feel like you're in this relationship.

  • You see the world through a different lens.
  • You notice the synchronicities and the opportunities.
  • You stop pushing away people who are the perfect fit, because you resonate with them at the emotional level.

This is what lets things unfold.
