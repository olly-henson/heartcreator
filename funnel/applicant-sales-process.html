Applicant Sales Process

Frankie Fihn's video selling method, taken from his full workshop transcript and mapped onto your funnel. Each step shows what he does, what we do and the exact messages to send. Three decisions are left, with my pick on each.

Frankie's 5 steps

Hand RaiserThey say "I'm interested". If they won't raise a hand, they won't buy
Pre-Sell the Plan"If you want X, this is what I'd do. What do you think?"
Loom VideoOutcome, who it works for, the steps, price, how to buy
Follow-UpGo deeper on one part of the plan. Never "did you see my message?"
Cash InShow them exactly how to pay and what happens next

Every step is the same message with more detail. His analogy is introducing a friend to someone single. First "would you like to meet her?", then "come to my barbecue on Sunday", then the time, the place and what to wear.

"If they won't raise their hand for it, they won't buy it."
