Why you're stuck in your head

There's a name for this. Intellectualization is the psychiatrist's term for a coping mechanism. When love feels uncomfortable, your mind goes up into analysing, searching and fixing, to get away from the feeling.

In this mode your brainwaves speed up into high beta, the fast, alert pattern linked to worry. The default mode network, the part of your brain behind your inner chatter, gets loud and self-critical.

In your head
High beta. Loud inner voice. Scanning, worrying, catastrophizing. Fight or flight.
In your heart
Low beta and alpha. Quieter mind. Present, light and playful. Nervous system in balance.

Your analytical mind is a real strength in . In love it turns everything into box-ticking and keeps you from feeling anything.
