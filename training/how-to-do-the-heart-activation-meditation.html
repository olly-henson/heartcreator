Heart Activation MeditationHow To Do It
This meditation helps you regulate your nervous system,
feel more like yourself and create a sense of wholeness
within you. It puts you in the right energy to attract
your ideal partner, and a better quality of life.

Here's how to do it.

1
Heart Awareness
Place a hand over the centre of your chest and let your attention settle there.
Image: HAM Inhale.jpg
2
Inhale
Breathe into your heart slowly, counting as you go. One thousand and one, one thousand and two...
Image: HAM Default.png
3
Pause
Hold the breath for a moment. No strain, just a soft, natural pause.
Image: HAM Exhale.jpg
4
Exhale
Release the breath slowly out through your heart, counting the same way.
Image: HAM Default.png
5
Settle
Once the breath is fully out, let your body settle before the next one. Repeat the cycle.
Image: Remember.png
6
Remember
This practice is going to physically move you into Heart Coherence.
