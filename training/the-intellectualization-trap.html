Where It Starts

The Problem: Intellectualization

Intellectualization is a term psychiatrists use for trying to think our way out of an uncomfortable feeling. When we haven’t found a quality relationship for a while, we start to doubt it will ever happen for us. That doubt feels uncomfortable, so we decide something must be wrong with us. Our analytical mind then goes into overdrive, trying to ‘fix’ it.
