Image: Meditation Step 1.png
1
Close Your Eyes
Settle into a comfortable position and gently close your eyes. You can perform this meditation either sitting up or lying down. Just keep a neutral spine position.
Image: Meditation Step 2.png
2
Relax
Let go of any tension, softening from head to toe.
Image: Meditation Step 3.png
3
Notice Your Heart
Bring your attention into the centre of your chest and place your focus on your heart. Allow any thoughts or distractions to come and go as you keep your attention on your heart.
Image: Meditation Step 4.png
4
Heart-Focused Breathing
Breathe a little slower and deeper into your heart and on the out breath release the breath.
Image: Meditation Step 5.png
5
Intention
Remember your intention, recall your symbol.
Image: Meditation Step 6.png
6
Feel
As the feelings come up as you recall your intention, breathe this feeling into your heart a little slower and deeper than normal and release the feeling on the out breath.
Image: Meditation Step 7.png
7
Surrender
Let go of how or when, and hand it over to a power greater than yourself.
Image: Meditation Step 8.png
8
Give Thanks
Give thanks to a power greater than yourself that you are now living in this reality.
