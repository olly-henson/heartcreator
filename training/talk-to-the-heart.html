What It Is

Mind And Heart Coherence

Your mind and your heart agree on what you want.

When they’re aligned about what you want to change or create, it starts to feel real and normal to you.

Talk To Your Heart

How
1Act like you’re having a conversation with your heart
2Talk to it in the present tense
3State what you already have
4Keep your attention on your heart as you do it

Present Tense

Say It Like You Already Have It

Not Like This“I Want…”“I want to meet someone who loves me.” Your heart hears that you don’t have it yet.Still waiting
Like This“I Have…”“I have a partner who loves me unconditionally.” Your heart feels it as if it’s real now.Already yours

Examples

What You Might Say

Use your own intentions, in the present tense.

“I have a partner who is emotionally available.”
“We share the same values and a future together.”
“I feel safe, relaxed and playful with them.”
“We’re ready to marry and build a family together.”
