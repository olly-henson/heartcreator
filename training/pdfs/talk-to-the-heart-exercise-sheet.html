Talk To The Heart

Get your mind and your heart to agree on what you want.

Your mind and your heart agree on what you want.

That’s mind and heart coherence. Talking to your heart imprints your subconscious with what you want.

The StepsHow To Do It

1
Close Your EyesClose your eyes and bring your attention to your heart.
2
Hand Over Your HeartPlace your hand or two fingers over your heart.
3
Talk To Your HeartLike a conversation. In the present tense, state what you already have. Keep your attention on your heart.
4
Let It Settle In YouNo need to breathe in or out in any special way. Just let it settle.
5
Carry On With Your DayOpen your eyes and carry on with your day.

Present TenseSay It Like You Already Have It

Not like this“I want to meet someone who loves me.”
Like this“I have a partner who loves me unconditionally.”
