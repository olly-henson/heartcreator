Heart Creator

Create Your Outcome

Grab a pen and paper. Writing this out by hand makes it more powerful than typing it, so use this page as your guide, not the place you fill it in.
Keep the page you write on somewhere you'll see it. The act of writing it by hand is part of what makes it work.
Creation: Financial Abundance
1
Choose
Choose your creation
On your paper, write down what you want to create.
New Relationship Healing Financial Abundance New Job Better Health Business Growth
Creation: Financial Abundance
2
Symbolize
Turn it into your symbol
Pick a capital letter for your creation.
Draw it on your paper, then circle it with two wavy lines. The wavy lines represent the intention and the associated emotions.
You'll use this as your reference during the meditation.
