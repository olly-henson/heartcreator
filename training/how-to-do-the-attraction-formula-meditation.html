How To Perform The Meditation

A quick overview of how to perform the meditation.
Image: Step 1.png
1
Close Your Eyes
Settle into a comfortable position and gently close your eyes. You can perform this meditation either sitting up or lying down. Just keep a neutral spine position.
Image: Step 2.png
2
Relax
Let go of any tension, softening from head to toe.
Image: Step 3.png
3
Notice Your Heart
Bring your attention into the centre of your chest and place your focus on your heart. Allow any thoughts or distractions to come and go as you keep your attention on your heart.
Image: Step 4.png
4
Heart Breathing
Begin to breathe into your heart and on the out breath release the breath out of the heart.
Image: New Intention Step v2.jpg
5
Intention
Recall your symbol and work through and remember each of your intentions. Use the phrase 'My intention is that…' at the start of each of your intentions.
Image: Step 1.png
6
Remember
Where will you be and what will you be doing during your day?
Image: Step 7.png
7
Feel
How will you feel when this happens? Breathe that feeling in and out of your heart.
Image: Step 1.png
8
Give Thanks
Give thanks and feel the gratitude that this has happened for you.
