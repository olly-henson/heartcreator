Why These Exercises Work

They Quiet The Analytical Mind

Moving your attention away from everything going on in your life and focusing your collective attention on one thing slows down your analytical mind. Your brainwaves shift from high beta to alpha and theta. In this state, we have greater access to the subconscious mind. From this point you can hold clear intentions and maintain defined emotional states without losing your focus or becoming distracted.

Your Busy MindScattered Thinking about everything going on in your life. Hard to focus.
Brainwaves: high beta
After The ExercisesCoherent Your brain firing together. Focused on what you want, without distraction.
Brainwaves: alpha and theta

Notice Where Your Hand Is

How
1Keep your eyes open
2Place your hand over the centre of your chest
3Notice where your hand is
4Take your hand away
5Notice where your hand was

Exercise 1 · Step 2

Focus On A Spot On The Wall

How
1Sit at a desk or table
2Pick one small spot on the wall
3Rest your attention on it
4Feel like you're touching it with your mind. Soft and steady: not hard focus, not too light
5When your mind wanders, bring it back

How
