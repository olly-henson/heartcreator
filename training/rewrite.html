Internal Working Models
These are the unconscious beliefs about your own worthiness and whether others can be trusted, formed in childhood — and still running the show in every relationship since.
"I'm not worthy / not enough"
Rejecting genuine love, or being drawn to partners who confirm the belief by undervaluing you.
Becomes "I am worthy of love"
"I have to earn love"
Over-giving, over-functioning, performing to be kept rather than being loved as you are.
Becomes "I am loved unconditionally"
"If I need too much, I'll be too much"
Suppressing your needs, which quietly builds resentment and distance.
Becomes "My needs are valid"
"Others can't be trusted, people leave"
Guardedness, testing behavior, pushing people away before they can leave first.
Becomes "It's safe to let someone in"
"Closeness is dangerous"
Craving closeness while fearing it once it's within reach; pulling away right when things get real.
Becomes "Closeness is safe"
"I will be abandoned"
Constant monitoring for signs someone's pulling away, or pre-emptively pushing people out before they can leave first.
Becomes "I am safe"
"I have to manage everyone's emotions to be safe"
Regulating someone else's moods, de-escalating tension, walking on eggshells to keep the relationship calm.
Becomes "I am free to let others feel their own way"
"Closeness is fragile and temporary"
Even when you do get closeness, part of you braces for it to disappear rather than fully relaxing into it.
Becomes "Closeness can be stable and last"
